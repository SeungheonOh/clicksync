clicksync canonical fresh UTxO-flow dataset contract

dataset_manifest is an append-only MergeTree ordered by (manifest_key, revision)
with index_granularity 64. Its bounded authoritative head carries immutable
dataset/network/genesis/start identity, this contract hash, a client-verified
transition digest chain, physical/effective/floor event-point snapshots,
sampled-peer trust state, visibility generation, and pending rollback
reservation state.

blocks, transactions, inputs, outputs, datum_bodies, datum_observations,
withdrawals, redeemers, and transaction_metadata are immutable physical facts.
chain_events and rollbacks are append-only canonical membership events.
peer_observations are append-only trust evidence. writer_audit is operational
history and is not writer authority.

Visibility is determined by the manifest effective event snapshot and
append-only adoption/invalidation membership. Raw transaction or block CBOR,
script bodies, certificates, governance actions, votes, and proposals are not
part of this dataset.

The sampling cadence is 512 primary blocks. A physical suffix may contain at
most 767 blocks. checking and disputed trust clamp the effective head;
unavailable trust serves the physical head with explicit warning and lag.
